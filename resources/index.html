Community Engagement

Community engagement
resources

Remaking museums as agents of change, embracing diversity, deepening community engagement, and focusing on social justice.

— Kathleen McLean

The book's community engagement framework follows a ten-step process: clearly stated change and roles; context, demographics, and stakeholders; mapping (especially empathy mapping); audience segmentation; strategy including stakeholder analysis; tactics; KPIs and metrics; testing, evaluation, and iteration; marketing; and launch.
